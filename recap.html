The Voyage Home: Mahalo & Memories

On October 11, 2026, ten brave voyagers, one distant navigator, and one keiki deckhand set sail across the ocean to Carter Island. Guided only by the stars, the ocean swells, the land-finding birds, and the love of our kūpuna, our canoe reached shore, cracked the final chest, and welcomed our family's newest little voyager home!
